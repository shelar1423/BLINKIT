Racer price flows

Built on the current design to answer the Zomato feedback: the result decides a reward you can feel at the shelf, on the car, for a limited time. Each phone is the live app with its own state; tap inside any of them.

The rules
  1. Points climb one discount ladder: 1,000 → 5%, 2,500 → 10%, 5,000 → 15%, 8,000 → 20% off every Hot Wheels car, for 24 hours from the race that reached it. The leaderboard has its own prize: top 3 in each city when the 3-day drop ends win a District Pass.
  2. Cards: racer price in Blinkit's yellow ticket, shelf price struck, and a blue line turning between "10% off · Race reward" and "#9 on city leaderboard". The bill takes off the same, per car.
  3. Time left: one strip above the listing, not on every card.
  4. First landing after the race: listing with an empty cart → the bar; with items → no bar; home → the sheet. Once.
  5. After 24 hours, or once an order uses it, everything goes.
